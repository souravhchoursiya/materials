# 🎓 [Universal Study Portal - Student Guide](https://souravhchoursiya.github.io/materials/)

Welcome to the Department's Universal Study Portal! This platform provides you with secure, organized, and instant access to your course materials, lecture notes, and important documents.

## 🚀 How to Get Started

Using the portal is a simple two-step process. 

### Step 1: Register Your Account (First-Time Only)
Before you can download any materials, you must register your details in the system.
1. Open the portal and click on the **"Register Here"** tab.
2. Enter your **Full Name**.
3. Enter your **Roll Number** (Make sure there are no typos, as this will act as your password).
4. Click **"Complete Registration"**. 
*Once successful, you will automatically be redirected to the access page.*

### Step 2: Access & Download Notes
Whenever you need to study, use your Roll Number to unlock your files.
1. Click on the **"Unlock Notes"** tab.
2. Select your **Course Category** (e.g., DSC, DSE) from the first dropdown menu.
3. Select your specific **Subject** from the second dropdown menu.
4. Enter your registered **Roll Number**.
5. Click **"Access Material"**.
6. You will instantly see a list of all available files for that subject. Click **"Open"** to view or download a file directly to your device.

---

## 🔒 Important Security Notes

* **Your Roll Number is your key:** Treat your Roll Number like a password. Do not share it with other students, as the system securely logs access based on this number.
* **Always Up-To-Date:** You do not need to refresh or download new versions of this portal. Whenever your professors upload new notes to the drive, they will automatically appear in your dropdown menus.

---

## 🙋‍♂️ Need Help?

If you experience any issues—such as your Roll Number not being recognized, a file not opening, or missing subjects—please reach out for technical support.

At the bottom of the portal, click the **"Need Help? Contact "** link. This will automatically open WhatsApp on your device with a pre-filled message directed to your Class Representative.
